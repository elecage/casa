per account per month, raises threshold alerts, and exports the totals for the
billing team. `README.md` has the layout and the document list.

finish everything in one go.

left, and what the next person should look at first. Only add to the
"decisions" section; don't remove what's already there. Several people work on
this repo in turns, so if you don't write down what you decided, the next
person has to dig through the code.
