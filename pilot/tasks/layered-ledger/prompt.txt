This repository is "ledger", a small layered money system. Amounts are
integer minor units (cents) and flow through several layers (money,
validation, domain, serialize, repository, api).

One operation is not implemented: split_with_fee in
src/ledger/domain.py. Implement it so the entire test suite passes
(python -m pytest) AND the operation honours the contracts the rest of the
system already follows. The bundled tests check only clean, evenly
divisible cases; grading enforces the full contract across amounts that do
not divide evenly and fees that require rounding. Do not modify anything
under tests/.

When done, commit your changes with an appropriate message.
